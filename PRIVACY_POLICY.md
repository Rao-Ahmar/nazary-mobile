# Privacy Policy

**Nazary — Travel & Tour Booking Platform**
Last updated: May 13, 2026

Nazary ("we", "our", "us") operates the Nazary mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Name, email address, and password
- Phone number (Pakistan format)
- Profile photo
- City

### Planner-Specific Information
If you register as a trip planner, we additionally collect:
- Agency name, tagline, and logo
- Cover photo
- Years of experience
- CNIC (National Identity Card) number for verification
- Bio and guild affiliation
- Social media URLs (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter, website)

### Trip & Booking Data
- Trip details (destination, dates, pricing, images)
- Booking history and status
- Trip reviews and ratings (1-5 stars and text)
- Trip preferences (budget range, preferred travel months)
- Trip requests (destination, travel dates, number of seats, special requirements)

### Payment Information
- Payment method type (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or Bank Transfer)
- Account title and account number (for planners receiving payments)
- Bank name (for bank transfers)
- Payment proof screenshots and reference numbers
- Transaction amounts

### Device & Technical Data
- Push notification tokens (Expo Push / Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- Device type and operating system

### User-Generated Content
- Photos uploaded for trips, profiles, and payment proofs
- Feedback submissions (subject and message)
- Place reviews

### Referral Data
- Referral codes and referral relationships
- Points and rewards history

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Connect travelers with verified trip planners
- Process and track trip bookings
- Send push notifications about booking updates, trip reminders, and new trips
- Display planner profiles and trip listings
- Verify planner identity and credentials
- Process referral bonuses and reward points
- Respond to feedback and support requests
- Improve app functionality and user experience

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Google Sign-In** | Account authentication via Google OAuth 2.0 |
| **Expo Push Notifications** | Delivering push notifications to your device |
| **Firebase Cloud Messaging** | Secondary push notification delivery |
| **AWS S3** | Secure storage of uploaded images and files |

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- Your password is encrypted using bcrypt and is never stored in plain text.
- Authentication tokens (JWT) are used for secure session management.
- Password reset tokens expire after 2 hours.
- All API communication uses HTTPS encryption.
- Images are stored securely via cloud storage services.
- Payment information is stored only to facilitate bookings between travelers and planners. We do not process payments directly.

## 5. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device:
- Authentication tokens (access and refresh tokens)
- User profile information for offline access
- Theme preferences (light/dark mode)
- App tour/onboarding completion status

This data is stored using secure local storage and can be cleared by logging out or uninstalling the app.

## 6. Permissions We Request

| Permission | Reason |
|------------|--------|
| **Camera** | To take photos for trips and profile pictures |
| **Photo Library** | To upload profile pictures and trip images |
| **Push Notifications** | To send booking updates, trip reminders, and new trip alerts |

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be visible to other users in the following ways:
- **Planners**: Your name and booking details are visible to the planner you book with.
- **Travelers**: Planner profiles, agency info, ratings, and reviews are publicly visible.
- **Reviews**: Trip reviews you write are visible to other users.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data through your profile settings
- **Update** your information at any time via the app
- **Delete your account** — contact us and we will remove your data
- **Opt out** of push notifications through your device settings

## 9. Children's Privacy

Nazary is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
